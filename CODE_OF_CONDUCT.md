# Code of conduct

## The short version

This repository publishes numbers about products people sell. Some of those numbers will be
unwelcome, including ours. Argue with the number, the method or the definition. Do not argue
with the person.

## Our standards

**Expected**

- Dispute a figure on its evidence: the table, the row, the versions, what you ran and what
  you got.
- State your affiliation when you have one. Working for a vendor whose row you are disputing
  does not weaken the dispute. Concealing it and having it emerge later does.
- Accept a decision that goes against you, and use the one appeal the disputes process gives
  you rather than reopening the argument in a new issue.
- Assume a mistake before assuming bad faith, including about us. Most wrong numbers are bugs.

**Not acceptable**

- Personal attacks, insults, or comments about someone's competence rather than their claim.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private, including sustained interruption of a thread
  after being asked to stop.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of a sexual nature.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sockpuppeting, brigading, or coordinating pressure on a maintainer to change or withdraw a
  figure.
- Pressure of any kind — commercial, legal or personal — to alter, delay or remove a result
  outside the published disputes process. That process is the only route, and it is the same
  route for everybody, including us.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages of this
repository, and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct that breaches this document to **hello@entailmentlabs.com**. Reports are read
by the benchmark owner. If the report concerns the benchmark owner, say so and it is handled
by a founder.

We will acknowledge the report, look into it, and tell the reporter what we decided. We will
not disclose the reporter's identity to the person reported without the reporter's agreement.

Consequences, in the order we would normally reach for them:

| Response | When |
|---|---|
| A private note explaining what was wrong | A first, minor breach |
| A public correction in the thread, with an apology requested | A breach visible to others |
| A temporary block from the repository | A repeated breach, or one serious enough on its own |
| A permanent block | A pattern of breaches, harassment, or any attempt to intimidate a contributor or a maintainer |

A block never removes a dispute from the log. A dispute raised by someone who is later
blocked is still decided on its evidence and still published, because the log is about the
figure and not about the person.
